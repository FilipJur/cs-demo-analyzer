# Annotated Sources — Modern CS2 Meta (Oct 2024 – Oct 2026)

Columns: author · title · URL · publication date · patch period · evidence type ·
limitations. Evidence grades: **A** primary (peer-reviewed / named first-person
pro/coach) · **B** analyst data (named analyst, positions-derived metrics) ·
**C** practitioner guide (definition-reliable, not efficacy evidence) ·
**S** secondary (dataset/editorial, method disclosed) · **V** Valve/verbatim patch
record. `[pre-window]` / `[unverified-transfer]` / `[lead — re-verify]` as in
`concepts.md`.

Parent-verification note: `counter-strike.net/news/updates`, Steam news feeds and
Liquipedia were JS-/access-blocked during research; Valve notes were verified via
cs2metahistory verbatim transcriptions carrying Valve's Steam announcement links.
HLTV was intermittently HTTP-429; blocked articles are leads, not evidence.
