# Verifiable Cases — Modern CS2 Meta (Oct 2024 – Oct 2026)

Every case names teams/players, map, event, date, and a round/timestamp or
dataset anchor plus a retrievable source. **Nothing is invented.** Where a scout
read the source directly the citation is firm; where HLTV rate-limiting blocked
re-fetch, the case carries `[lead — re-verify]` and is NOT cited as evidence.
Pre-window CS2 cases (mid-2024) carry `[pre-window]`. Each case links to concept
IDs in `concepts.md` and states what it does / does not prove.

### CASE-01 — NAVI conditioning payoff vs G2, EWC 2024 final (Nuke, R4) `[pre-window]`

- **Match:** NAVI vs G2, Esports World Cup 2024 grand final, 21–22 Jul 2024.
  T side NAVI (Aleksib, iM, w0nderful, jL, b1t).

  utility, then hit Squeaky/Hut after the CT molly expires as G2 rotates away —

### CASE-10 — Falcons' Cologne Major 2026 title run (Jun 2026)

- **Event:** IEM Cologne Major 2026. Falcons (karrigan, NiKo, TeSeS, m0NESY,

- **Sources:** Winio "IEM Cologne Major 2026: What Falcons' Title Really Revealed",

  2026-07-14 (both secondary, method-disclosed; results cross-checkable on HLTV).

## Roles / roster-construction cases

### CASE-11 — HEROIC tN1R: forced lurk → spacetaker switch (mid-2025)

- **Change:** Coach TOBIZ moves tN1R from lurking to star/spacetaker roles.
- **Cost:** T-side rating 1.19 (2025 H1) → 0.93 (from 2025-07-01); opening attempts
  16.5% → 23.3% while opening kills fall 0.09 → 0.07/round. Player: "very hard…
  I don't have experience in these roles… we don't have solo practice."
- **Shows:** META-R09 fluidity ≠ interchangeability; a **deliberate, reasoned
  decision with a measured individual cost** — outcome-bias guardrail case.
- **Source:** NER0cs, HLTV 2025-07-31 (data + embedded quote).
- **Does not prove:** the switch was wrong — coach rationale (communication load) is outside the demo.

## Successful decisions that ended badly / poor decisions that succeeded

- **Good process, bad outcome:** CASE-06 (xertioN's correct calls losing to Spirit
  retakes before converting); CASE-11 (reasoned tN1R switch costing 0.26 rating);
  CASE-17 (MongolZ's pre-plant style producing the worst retake number).
- **Bad process, good outcome:** CASE-09 M2R28-inverted — sh1ro's ACE is elite
  execution that still loses on timer (execution ≠ decision); any dry retake in the
  1.8–7.8% cells that converts on hero shots (Soarne doctrine: "the decision was
  still bad" — `[guide]` 2026-07-07); CASE-04-inverted — fnatic's 4-man B-crunch
  force-buy win on Train (same series) is success that must not bless the method.
- **Review rule for all of the above:** re-run the decision from the information
  state, never from the scoreboard (S-G01).
